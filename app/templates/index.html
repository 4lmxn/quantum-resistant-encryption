Each node agrees its own key with the server using ML-KEM-768 (CRYSTALS-Kyber, NIST FIPS 203). The key itself never crosses the wire — both sides derive it independently, and the fingerprints above are SHA-256 digests proving they landed on the same one. The thermometer and the fan hold different keys, so breaking one leg gives you nothing on the other. Messages are sealed with AES-256-GCM, whose authentication tag is the wax seal the fan checks.
